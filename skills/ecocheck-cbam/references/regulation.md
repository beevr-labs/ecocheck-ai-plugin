## Free-allocation phase-out (CBAM factor = share of free allocation kept)

| Year | 2026 | 2027 | 2028 | 2029 | 2030 | 2031 | 2032 | 2033 | 2034 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CBAM factor | 97.5 % | 95 % | 90 % | 77.5 % | 51.5 % | 39 % | 26.5 % | 14 % | 0 % |

Watch: proposal COM(2026) 616 (July 2026) would change the phase-out after 2028; proposal COM(2025) 989 would add ~180 downstream products from 2028. Neither is law yet.

Values in the Commission workbooks are informational; the Regulations are binding. Re-check this file when the acts change.
