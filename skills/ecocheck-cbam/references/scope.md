## Traps

- Not CBAM goods: ferrous waste and scrap (7204), ferro-silicon (7202 21), ferro-silico-manganese (7202 30), most other ferro-alloys, articles of chapter 73 outside the listed headings (e.g. 7323 kitchenware, 7324 sanitary ware, 7325 cast articles, 7312-7317, 7319-7325), aluminium articles outside 7603-7614 / 7616.
- Scope is decided on the 8-digit CN code of the import declaration; a heading-level "yes" (e.g. 7326) still needs the exact code to find the default value.
- Downstream extension: proposal COM(2025) 989 (17 December 2025) would add about 180 steel- and aluminium-intensive downstream products from 1 January 2028. It is not law until adopted — mention it as a risk, not an obligation.
- Origin EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Switzerland: CBAM does not apply.
