# Base year and recalculation

## Choosing a base year

- The earliest year with complete, verifiable data for categories 1 and 2 (and significant 3-6).
- Explain the choice; a single year or a multi-year average are both possible.
- For Vietnam's Decree 06 cycle, many facilities use their first reported year (e.g. 2024 or 2025) as base year.

## Recalculation policy (write it before you need it)

Recalculate the base year (and intermediate years) when one of these changes the base-year total by more than the significance threshold (commonly 5 %, cumulative):

1. Structural change: acquisition, divestment, merger, insourcing / outsourcing of emitting activities.
2. Methodology change: new factors (e.g. switching IPCC defaults to national factors), GWP set change, better activity data.
3. Discovery of significant errors.

Do not recalculate for organic growth or decline (production up / down), or for facilities built or closed after the base year.

## Year-on-year comparison

- Compare absolute tCO2e and an intensity (tCO2e per tonne of product, per billion VND revenue, per m²).
- Explain changes by driver: production volume, energy mix, efficiency projects, factor updates (separate "real" from "methodological" change).
