# Boundaries and reporting categories

## Organizational boundary (which facilities are in)

| Approach | Include | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| Operational control | 100 % of sites whose operating policies the organization sets | Default for most manufacturers; matches Decree 06 facility thinking |
| Financial control | 100 % of sites whose financial / operating policies it directs for economic benefit | Groups aligned with financial consolidation |
| Equity share | Its share of each site's emissions | Joint ventures, holding companies |

State the approach, list every site (address, activity) and explain exclusions (e.g. sold during the year → pro-rata).

## Reporting categories (ISO 14064-1:2018) and cross-walk

| ISO category | Content | GHG Protocol |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Direct emissions and removals | Stationary combustion (boilers, kilns, generators), mobile combustion (own vehicles, forklifts), industrial processes (calcination, chemical reactions), fugitive (refrigerants, SF6, CH4 leaks, wastewater on site), land use | Scope 1 |
| 2 Indirect from imported energy | Purchased electricity, steam, heat, cooling, compressed air | Scope 2 (location-based; market-based if contractual instruments exist) |
| 3 Indirect from transportation | Upstream and downstream transport of goods, business travel, employee commuting, client / visitor transport | Scope 3 cat. 4, 6, 7, 9 |
| 4 Indirect from products the organization uses | Purchased goods and services, capital goods, fuel- and energy-related activities (WTT, T&D losses), waste disposal, upstream leased assets | Scope 3 cat. 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 |
| 5 Indirect from the use of the organization's products | Processing and use of sold products, end-of-life, downstream leased assets, investments | Scope 3 cat. 10, 11, 12, 13, 15 |
| 6 Other indirect | Anything not covered above (e.g. franchises) | Scope 3 cat. 14 / other |

EcoCheck uses this default mapping (a user may override a source, e.g. waste transport in cat. 3 instead of 4 — document it).

## The 15 GHG Protocol Scope 3 categories

Upstream: 1 Purchased goods and services · 2 Capital goods · 3 Fuel- and energy-related activities · 4 Upstream transportation and distribution · 5 Waste generated in operations · 6 Business travel · 7 Employee commuting · 8 Upstream leased assets.
Downstream: 9 Downstream transportation and distribution · 10 Processing of sold products · 11 Use of sold products · 12 End-of-life treatment of sold products · 13 Downstream leased assets · 14 Franchises · 15 Investments.

## Typical source list for a Vietnamese factory

- Cat. 1: diesel (DO) for generators and fire pumps, FO / coal / biomass / LPG / natural gas for boilers and kilns, gasoline / diesel company vehicles, forklifts (LPG / diesel), refrigerant top-ups (R-22, R-32, R-410A, R-134a…), SF6 in switchgear, on-site wastewater treatment (CH4), process CO2 (lime, clinker, glass, ceramics).
- Cat. 2: EVN grid electricity (by meter / bill), purchased steam.
- Cat. 3-6 (if significant): raw materials (steel, plastics, chemicals, packaging), inbound and outbound logistics, waste to landfill / incineration, business flights, commuting buses, WTT and grid losses.

## Common boundary mistakes

- Counting own-generated electricity in category 2 (it is category 1 fuel).
- Counting electricity sold / exported to others as a reduction of category 2.
- Forgetting leased warehouses or offices under operational control.
- Mixing biogenic CO2 from biomass combustion into category 1 totals (report separately; CH4 and N2O from biomass stay in category 1).
