# GHG report (ISO 14064-1) — structure and review checklist

Paraphrased content list; check the purchased standard (clause 9) for exact requirements.

## Suggested structure

1. Organization: name, sites, activities, responsible person, intended users and purpose, report date, reporting period.
2. Boundaries: consolidation approach, list of sites, reporting boundary (categories included), significance criteria and excluded sources with justification.
3. Results:
   - Totals by category 1-6 in tCO2e; category 1 split by gas (CO2, CH4, N2O, HFCs, PFCs, SF6, NF3) and by source type (stationary, mobile, process, fugitive, land use).
   - Category 2 location-based (and market-based where applicable).
   - Biogenic CO2 reported separately; removals reported separately.
   - Cross-walk to Scope 1/2/3 if requested (and Scope 3 by the 15 categories).
   - Intensity metrics (per product tonne / revenue / m²).
4. Methodology: quantification approaches per source, activity data sources, emission factors with references and year, GWP set, unit conversions, changes since the previous report.
5. Base year: year chosen, base-year totals, recalculation policy and any recalculation done.
6. Uncertainty and data quality: method, results, main contributors, improvement plan.
7. Reduction actions and targets (if any), clearly separated from the inventory.
8. Verification: whether the report is verified, verifier, level of assurance, statement reference.
9. Annexes: activity data tables, factor tables, organizational chart, list of evidence.

## Review checklist (run before sending to a verifier or customer)

- [ ] Reporting period and boundary stated; all sites under the chosen control approach listed.
- [ ] Categories 1 and 2 complete for every site and every month (no missing meters / bills).
- [ ] Units consistent (kWh vs MWh, L vs m³, kg vs t); conversion factors documented.
- [ ] No double counting (own generation vs purchased power; fuel in category 1 and WTT in category 4 counted once each as intended).
- [ ] Refrigerant top-ups from service records included; gases with correct GWP set.
- [ ] Biogenic CO2 and removals reported separately.
- [ ] Every emission factor has a source and year; Vietnam grid factor = latest official notice (cite it).
- [ ] Exclusions justified with significance results.
- [ ] Base year and recalculation policy stated; comparison with previous year explained by driver.
- [ ] Uncertainty statement present.
- [ ] Figures in summary tables equal the detailed tables (rounding only).
