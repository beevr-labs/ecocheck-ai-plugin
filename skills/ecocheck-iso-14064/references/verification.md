# Verification (ISO 14064-3) — what to prepare

ISO 14064-3 sets how a verifier (ideally accredited under ISO 14065 by a recognised accreditation body) plans and performs validation / verification of GHG statements.

## Key notions

- **Level of assurance**: reasonable (positive opinion, more evidence) or limited (negative assurance, "nothing has come to our attention"). Customers, banks and ETS schemes state which they need.
- **Materiality**: the verifier sets a quantitative threshold, commonly 5 % of the total (lower, e.g. 2 %, for large emitters or ETS), plus qualitative materiality (e.g. a missing site).
- **Process**: agreement on objectives, criteria, scope and level of assurance → strategic analysis and risk assessment → verification plan and sampling → evidence gathering (site visit, data trails, recalculation) → findings and corrections → verification statement.

## Evidence pack (have it ready)

| Item | Examples |
|---|---|
| Boundary | Organization chart, list of sites, ownership / control evidence, leases |
| Activity data | EVN bills, meter readings, fuel invoices and delivery notes, fuel logs, ERP extracts, refrigerant service reports, production records |
| Factors | National factor notices, IPCC tables, supplier certificates, lab analyses (NCV, carbon content) |
| Calculations | Workbook / software export with formulas traceable from source to total |
| Controls | Data owner per source, review and approval steps, change log, calibration certificates |
| Report | Draft GHG report with all clause 9 elements |

## Typical findings to pre-empt

Missing months, unit errors, wrong grid factor year, refrigerant omissions, double-counted generators, undocumented exclusions, spreadsheet errors (hard-coded numbers), base year not recalculated after an acquisition.

## Vietnam

For facilities in the Decree 06 list, the inventory report follows the national template and sector guidance; verification ("thẩm định") of facility inventories is organised under the Decree (for the pilot ETS facilities, a verified inventory goes to the Ministry of Agriculture and Environment). A voluntary ISO 14064-3 verification by an accredited body is separate and is what most international customers ask for.
