# Vietnam specifics for product footprints

## Electricity

- National grid emission factor for 2024: **0.6187 t CO2/MWh** (Official Letter 1232/BNNMT-BĐKH, Ministry of Agriculture and Environment, 30/09/2026). Use the latest published year that is not after the reference year of the study; earlier years: 0.6592 (2023), 0.6766 (2022), 0.7221 (2021) t CO2/MWh — check the official letter of each year.
- Rooftop solar used on site: only the grid electricity actually imported carries the grid factor; exported surplus is not a credit in the PCF.
- Green electricity claims (I-REC, DPPA under Decree 57/2025/NĐ-CP) only with certificates retired for the same period and consumption.

## Purchased CBAM goods as inputs

- Billets, wire rod, aluminium ingots, clinker, fertilisers bought from Vietnamese producers: until the supplier provides actual specific embedded emissions, the EU CBAM default value for Vietnam (Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2621 as corrected by 2026/1740) is a transparent, conservative proxy — say it is a default value, not supplier data. Note that CBAM covers direct emissions only for iron, steel and aluminium, so a cradle-to-gate PCF of the input may be higher.
- Ask suppliers for their PCF (PACT format if possible) or at least their CBAM SEE with the production route.

## Background data

- There is no public Vietnamese national LCA database; secondary data come from international databases (e.g. ADEME Base Empreinte, UK DESNZ conversion factors, USLCI, IPCC defaults). State the geography of each factor and rate the geographical representativeness accordingly; prefer factors for Asia / similar grids for energy-intensive processes.
- Transport in Vietnam: trucks are often loaded above European averages; when the customer can give tonne-km and vehicle type, use them.

## Who asks and why

- EU and Japanese/Korean buyers (automotive, electronics, apparel, packaging) ask suppliers for cradle-to-gate PCFs, often through PACT-compatible portals.
- Construction products sold into Europe or green-building projects ask for EPDs (EN 15804+A2).
- From 2026, EU CBAM importers ask steel, aluminium, cement and fertiliser exporters for embedded emissions — a different number from a PCF (see `documents.md`).
